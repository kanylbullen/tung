# Critical review of the phone app, 2026-10-29

A critical test of Tung on the owner's phone, with proposals for UX,
features and design. Read it as a reviewer's opinion, not as a plan: every
item names its evidence so it can be checked, and the order is one
reviewer's ranking.

## What was tested, and how

- **Build:** dogfood of `main` at 5aeb3a2 (versionCode 11, targetSdk 36),
  the first build with #97 (Live Update) and #98 (connect-client 1.1.0).
- **Device:** Pixel 11 Pro, Android 17 (SDK 37), the owner's real data
  (138 imported Jefit workouts, routines from the import and the
  assistant, an account on tung.xuper.fun). Driven over adb from COLE with
  taps, swipes, screenshots and uiautomator dumps; the English locale.
- **Walked:** Home (empty, with a workout in progress, scrolled), the
  Routines, Exercises, History and Progress tabs, a routine's page, a
  workout's detail, an exercise's page, every Settings page, the Assistant
  screen; the core loop: Start workout, Add exercise, the weight and reps
  sheets, tick, rest timer with Skip/+30 s, the progress sheet, the
  exercise menu, swipe-to-reveal Delete, Discard, Finish, the summary, and
  Delete from the detail. The test workout was finished and then deleted,
  so only tombstones remain.
- **Also:** a read of the screens' source and the two string files, and a
  comparison of the feature set with Strong, Hevy, Jefit, FitNotes,
  Liftin', Boostcamp and Alpha Progression (two Opus subagents; their
  claims were spot-checked in the code before being used here, and each
  item below says where its evidence comes from: *phone*, *code* or
  *comparison*).
- **Not tested:** the watch, sync under failure, the import flow itself
  (undo would have deleted 138 workouts), voice on the device, the Swedish
  locale on the device, predictive back's animation, the swipe's feel by
  hand, Health Connect writing.

## Verdict

The core of the app is excellent and nothing crashed. The weight sheet,
the sticky Log set button, set-by-set pre-fill, the muscle map and the
summary are better than the established apps at the one thing that
matters between sets. The Live Update works on Android 17. The weaknesses
are around that core, not in it: history that cannot be edited, deletes
without undo, gestures nobody will find, a rest timer that lives at the
top of a tall screen, copy that drifted, and a catalogue that shows its
two origins. Most of the fixes are small.

## What is strong and should stay as it is

- **The input sheet** (*phone*). A 96 sp number, a ruler that snaps and
  jumps, thumb-sized −/+ that follow the local step, typing that always
  wins, "Apply to all sets". This is the app's best screen.
- **The Log set bar** (*phone*). It always says what it will do and to what
  ("Log set · Bench press · 30 × 10"), never adds a set behind your back,
  and becomes "Add set" when there is nothing left to log.
- **Pre-fill** (*phone*). Bench press came in with five sets at last
  session's numbers; nothing opened. A routine workout with history is
  N + 4 taps.
- **The muscle map and the summary** (*phone*). The body with the worked
  muscles and the sets-per-muscle bars are honest numbers and read well.
- **The Live Update** (*phone*). The status-bar chip shows the app's icon
  and "30×10", and the notification sits on top of the shade with Log set
  and Finish; the system set `PROMOTED_ONGOING`.
- **Design discipline** (*phone*, *code*). One radius, no cards in cards,
  no dividers, no all-caps, no raw colours, tokens with contrast notes,
  English and Swedish with the same 622 keys. Settings and Account are
  calm.
- **Accessibility** (*code*). Delete is a custom action on every part of a
  set row, current and ticked states are described, charts have
  one-sentence summaries, every touch target but one is 48 dp.

## Defects and inconsistencies seen on the phone

These are things that are wrong today, as opposed to proposals.

1. **The Live Update's two texts disagree in a free workout.** The chip
   says "30×10" (the current set) while the body says "0 sets logged" and
   never names the exercise. `ongoingStatus` only answers "Bench press ·
   set 1 of 5" when the exercise has a routine target
   (`core/.../OngoingStatus.kt:59-64`); without one it falls back to the
   count. Proposal: use `AtSet` with the exercise's own set count when
   there is no target, so a free workout reads "Bench press · set 1 of 5"
   too. S.
2. **The finish dialog's buttons stack vertically** ("Finish workout" over
   "Keep training") because the labels do not fit side by side, and the
   title repeats the confirm label. Proposal: "Finish?" as the title,
   "Finish" and "Keep training" as buttons, and say what goes: "4 sets
   that are not ticked will be removed." S.
3. **Duplicate routines.** The Routines tab lists "Your Adaptive Plan ·
   Back & Shoulders Workout" twice and "Full Body Workout" twice, with no
   way to tell them apart. The import makes identical days once, so these
   came from two imports or two "Save program" taps; nothing warns or
   merges. Proposal: on save and import, detect a routine with the same
   name and the same exercises and ask "Replace Push day?"; and a "Delete
   duplicates" action in selection mode. S–M.
4. **The catalogue shows its two origins.** Core names are sentence case
   ("Bench press"), the 600 extended ones Title Case ("Alternating Floor
   Press", "Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl"), side by side in the Exercises
   tab, the picker and the Progress rows; and the source's data has holes
   ("Bodyweight Flyes · EZ bar"). Proposal: sentence-case the extended
   names in `import_free_exercise_db.py` (keep proper nouns), and let the
   alias and equipment tables correct the worst entries. S.
5. **Progress rows have three different second halves:** "+2 kg in 3
   months", "No change in 3 months" (wraps to two lines, with a flat
   sparkline) and "29 sessions" (no sparkline). Proposal: one shape per
   row: the latest value, then the change or "1 session in 3 months", and
   always a sparkline, even of one point. S.
6. **"30 × 10 is no new record" is in the accent colour**, the colour of
   "New record" and "Finish workout". Neutral and bad news in the positive
   colour is the pattern in several places (see 9 below). S.
7. **The metric chooser is a dropdown everywhere on this phone** (Progress
   tab, the sheet, the summary), so the chips "Est. 1RM · Volume · Weekly
   volume" are never seen; the owner's font scale is above 1. The fallback
   works, but the design intent (chips) does not survive a common setting.
   Proposal: shorter labels ("1RM · Volume · Weekly"), or make the dropdown
   the design and style it as one. S.
8. **"Units: Kilograms (kg)" looks like a setting and does nothing**
   (`ui/settings/TrainingScreen.kt:97-100`). Proposal: "Kilograms (pounds
   are not supported yet)", or drop the row until lb exists. S.

## Proposals, ranked

### A. The core loop

1. **Edit a finished workout** (*phone*, *code*). The detail shows the sets
   as plain text and the menu has only "Save as routine" and "Delete
   workout". A wrong weight, a set that was forgotten, or a workout that
   was not finished until the next morning (its duration is wrong) can
   only be fixed by deleting the whole workout. Forgetting to tick the last
   set is the most common slip, and Finish deletes it. Proposal: a tap on a
   set in the detail opens the same `NumberInputSheet` (`SetWeight` and
   `SetReps` take any set id already), plus delete set and a
   `SetWorkoutTimes` command; and "Log a past workout" from History. The
   server already accepts `PUT /workouts/{id}`. **M.**
2. **Undo on every delete of training data** (*phone*, *code*). "Remove
   exercise" sits under "Rest timer…" in a two-item menu and runs at once
   with its ticked sets (`ActiveWorkoutScreen.kt:900`); the Delete a swipe
   reveals has no undo either; "Undo last import" in Settings → Data
   deletes every imported workout with no confirmation
   (`ImportSection.kt:111`), while every other bulk delete asks. Proposal:
   a snackbar with Undo for five seconds on remove exercise and delete set
   (defer the command or restore the rows), and a confirmation on undo
   import: "Remove 138 imported workouts? Workouts you logged in Tung are
   kept." **S for the confirm, M for undo.**
3. **The rest timer where the thumb is** (*phone*). The countdown is a
   small accent number in the top bar next to the elapsed time; Skip and
   +30 s appear in a row under the top bar after a tap on it, and nothing
   on screen says that the tap exists. Between sets the countdown is the
   most important thing on the screen. Proposal: while resting, show it in
   the sticky bar above Log set, large and tabular, with Skip and +30 s
   beside it; the top bar keeps the elapsed time. **M.**
4. **Reorder and replace an exercise in a workout** (*comparison*,
   *code*). Routines can move exercises, workouts cannot, and the rack
   being taken is the everyday case. Proposal: `MoveWorkoutExercise(delta)`
   and `ReplaceExercise(workoutExerciseId, ref)` keeping the set count and
   pre-filling from the new exercise's history. **S.**
5. **Give the gestures an affordance** (*phone*, *code*). Swipe to reveal
   Delete, tap a set number to make it current, tap the header to switch
   exercise, tap the countdown for Skip, long-press a routine to select,
   hold the mic for hands-free: none has a visible cue, and supersets,
   deleting a set and bulk delete all hang on them. Proposal: "Delete set"
   and "Make current" in a long-press menu on the row, a "Select" action in
   the Routines top bar, a visible Skip next to the countdown, and
   one-time hints in the first workout ("Tap a set number to do it next").
   **M.**
6. **A visible current set** (*phone*). The marker is a 3 dp accent bar at
   x = 0 (`ActiveWorkoutScreen.kt:1036`), under a phone case's lip in poor
   light, and it is the only cue for what Log set will tick. Proposal: the
   `raised` background on the current row and the set number in the
   accent; keep the state description. **S.**
7. **Fewer taps for a new user** (*code*). Three exercises × three sets
   with no history is about 39 taps: the picker pops after every pick, the
   first-time flow is two sheets, the finish dialog always shows, and the
   summary has both ✕ and Done. Proposal: let the picker add several
   exercises before returning, put weight and reps in one first-time
   sheet, skip the finish dialog when nothing would be removed, drop the
   summary's ✕. **M.**
8. **"Save as routine" after a routine workout should update, not
   duplicate** (*code*; the duplicates on the phone are the symptom). When
   `workout.routineId != null`, make the summary's button "Update Push day"
   and move "Save as new routine" into the menu; a small
   `update_routine_from_workout` command. **M.**
9. **A colour for bad news** (*phone*, *code*). "Delete", "Discard",
   "Finish workout" in the confirm dialogs, "Wrong email or password", "is
   no new record" and "New record" are all the same yellow
   (`DiscardWorkoutDialog.kt:30`, `AccountScreen.kt:116`, the progress
   sheet). `danger` is fill only. Proposal: a `dangerText` token that
   passes 4.5:1 in both themes for destructive confirms and errors, muted
   text for neutral lines, accent only for good news and the primary
   action; add it to CLAUDE.md's token table. **S.**
10. **The sheet should show the number that matters** (*phone*). The
    full-height weight sheet hides the row's "Last: 80 × 8" for every row
    but the top one. Proposal: "Last: 80 × 8" under the sheet's title, and
    the progress sheet's record line when one applies ("8 reps would beat
    your estimate"). **S.**

### B. Around the core

11. **History rows say nothing but the date** (*phone*). "Thursday 22
    October · 25 min · 3 exercises · 2 430 kg": push day and leg day look
    the same until opened; no month headers, no year, no calendar.
    Proposal: the routine name (or the first two exercises) as the row's
    title with the date muted below, month headers, and later a month grid
    at the top. **M.**
12. **A workout in progress is invisible off Home** (*phone*). On the
    other tabs nothing says a workout is running, and every Start is a bare
    grey "Start" with no reason. Proposal: a strip above the nav bar on
    every tab, "Workout in progress · 23:10 · Resume", and either a muted
    reason under a disabled Start or turning it into Resume. **M.**
13. **Routines: group by package and dedupe** (*phone*). Every imported
    routine starts with its package ("Skokloster Gym · ", "Your Adaptive
    Plan · "), so names wrap and the eye scans the same prefix; the
    "Programs" row at the top has no affordance. Proposal: section headers
    per package (or the package muted on a second line), a chevron on
    Programs, and the dedupe from defect 3. **S–M.**
14. **Your exercises first** (*phone*). The Exercises tab is about 680
    rows with the user's own exercises buried among 600 never-used ones,
    and "Create exercise" comes after all of them
    (`ExercisesScreen.kt:227-233`). Proposal: a first section "Your
    exercises" (has a row or history), the catalogue after it, and with
    text in the search a first result row: Create "<query>". **S.**
15. **Progress: records up, empty state with a button** (*phone*,
    *code*). The Exercises list starts below the fold, and Recent records,
    the most motivating content, comes after every exercise row. Proposal:
    Recent records under the weekly bars, then the exercises; an empty
    state with "Start workout". **S.**
16. **Sync failures are invisible** (*code*). The Settings row says
    "Signed in as …" whatever `lastError` is (`SettingsScreen.kt:106-113`),
    and on Account the error replaces the last-sync time. Proposal: "Sync
    failing · last synced 3 Oct" in the row, the time kept under the error,
    and a quiet line on Home when the last success is over 24 h old.
    **S–M.**
17. **Buttons for errors that send you elsewhere** (*code*). "Allow it in
    the system settings", "Install or update Health Connect": correct, but
    no button. Proposal: "Open settings" and "Get Health Connect" next to
    the lines. **S.**
18. **Custom exercises can be fixed** (*code*). The create dialog silently
    picks Chest + Barbell (`ExercisesScreen.kt:397-398`), and there is no
    rename, edit or delete. Proposal: no preselection, Save disabled until
    group and equipment are chosen, and "Edit" on the exercise page through
    an `update_exercise` command. **M.**
19. **First run and the import's audience** (*code*). An empty Home is one
    button and "No workouts yet"; a switching Jefit user has to guess
    Settings → Data. Proposal: three quiet rows under the button on an
    empty Home: "Create a routine", "Import from Jefit", "Set up a
    program". **S.**
20. **The import screen** (*code*). "This is not a Jefit export" offers no
    way to pick another file; Import is a long scroll under 30 names; the
    bulk actions are chips that read as toggles. Proposal: "Choose another
    file" on errors, a sticky bottom bar "8 of 11 decided · Import", real
    buttons. **S.**
21. **The assistant's wait** (*code*). Up to 120 s of "Waiting for the
    answer…" with no cancel, and with a workout in progress "Start this
    workout" appends to it. Proposal: a progress indicator with Cancel, and
    "Add to current workout" as the label when one is in progress. **S.**
22. **Copy that drifted** (*code*; the Swedish file was read, not seen on
    the device). "Save a finished workout as a routine first" predates
    `create_routine` and is now false (`strings.xml:148, 363`); Swedish has
    "roterande" vs "Rullande", "Idag" vs "i dag", "repetitioner" vs "reps";
    "1Y" and "12M" for the same span; "Last: Never" capitalised; one
    contraction in the app ("Didn't catch that"). Proposal: align the
    terms, one label for twelve months, "Last: never", and a small "EN" tag
    on English-only catalogue content on a Swedish phone. **S.**
23. **Small things** (*code*): the routine target-reps row is the one
    touch target under 48 dp (`RoutineDetailScreen.kt:275`); a selected
    chip is inverted on some screens and accent-filled on others; the mic
    is a second accent element next to the one bold move and shows even
    without a recogniser; the sticky button's label can wrap to three
    lines with a long Swedish exercise name. **S each.**

## Feature gaps worth closing (from the comparison)

Only #5 (Strong and Hevy import) among the open issues is a feature gap,
so none of these is planned yet. Effort includes writing each command twice
(app and server) until #87 lands.

1. **Export and restore in the app.** Only the server has `GET /export`,
   and only for a signed-in user; `docs/architecture.md` promises "JSON
   export at any time". Move the export shape into core, add "Export
   (JSON)" and "Export sets (CSV)" to Settings → Data with
   `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`, and a `tung` import adapter so restore reuses
   `Importer`'s deterministic ids. **M.** Local first without this is a
   promise half kept.
2. **Edit a finished workout, log a past one** (A1 above). **M.**
3. **Reorder and replace in a workout** (A4). **S.**
4. **Warm-up sets.** Jefit's warm-ups come in as ordinary sets and inflate
   volume, the muscle breakdown and the weekly trend. A nullable
   `SetEntry.kind` (`warmup`, `drop`, `failure`); volume, records,
   `muscleBreakdown` and e1RM skip warm-ups; the set number shows a muted
   "W". **M.** This also makes a warm-up generator possible (S after it).
5. **A progression rule on routine exercises.** An optional
   `RoutineExercise.incrementKg`; a pure `progressedStartSets` adds it when
   last session hit every target set at the target reps, with a muted
   reason line "+2.5 kg: 3 × 8 done last time". Deterministic and shown,
   never silent, unlike the AI-driven plans of Fitbod and Alpha
   Progression. **M.**
6. **Plate calculator.** A pure `platesPerSide(target, bar, plates)`; the
   weight sheet shows "20 + 10 + 2.5 per side" for barbell and EZ-bar
   exercises; bar and plates in Settings → Training like My equipment. It
   is numbers first by definition. **S–M.**
7. **Notes.** `Workout.note` exists in Room and sync with no UI; a
   `SetWorkoutNote` command and a field on the summary and the detail
   (**S**), then a sticky note per exercise, "seat 4, narrow grip"
   (**M**).
8. **Pounds as a display unit.** Storage stays kg; convert at the edges
   (formatting, the ruler's 5 lb steps, `stepWeight`, voice, the prompt,
   the watch, the chip) and keep the exact kg of the lb input to avoid
   225 lb coming back as 224.9. **L**, but it decides reach outside Europe.
9. **Timed and distance sets.** Plank and farmer's walk are in the
   catalogue but a set is weight × reps. A `tracking` field in the
   catalogue and nullable `durationSeconds`/`distanceM` on `SetEntry`;
   scope it to strength accessories. **L.**
10. **Supersets as a grouping.** A nullable `groupId` on `WorkoutExercise`
    and `RoutineExercise`; `nextCurrentAfterLogging` alternates inside the
    group and the rest timer starts after the group's last exercise. **M–L.**

Smaller: RPE/RIR as an opt-in chip row (M), repeat a workout from its
detail (S), body weight read from Health Connect (S–M), assisted
bodyweight with negative load (M), a month grid in History (S–M), a Wear
tile with Today's routine (M), share a workout as text (S), a % of 1RM
table on exercise progress (S), muscles on custom exercises (M).

## What not to copy

Social feeds and leaderboards (they turn the server into a network and
fight Private mode), streak nags and confetti (motion only in response to
an action), background AI that rewrites plans (the AI is called only on a
tap and writes nothing without confirmation), "muscle recovery %" and MET
calorie estimates (false precision; the sets-per-muscle breakdown and
Health Connect's calories are honest), licensed GIF libraries (the
catalogue is CC0 and the APK is small), progress photos on the server, and
set rows with tempo, RPE and type on every row by default.

## A suggested order

1. **One small PR of fixes seen on the phone:** defects 1, 2, 5, 6, 8; the
   undo-import confirmation and a confirm on remove exercise with ticked
   sets (A2's S half); the stale "save a workout first" copy; the current
   row's background (A6). All S, all low risk.
2. **The rest timer in the sticky bar** (A3) and **affordances for the
   gestures** (A5): the two changes a tester would notice most in the gym.
3. **Edit a finished workout** (A1) and **undo** (A2's M half): the two
   that stop data loss.
4. **Export and restore** (gap 1): the local-first promise.
5. Then catalogue casing and dedupe, History rows, the in-progress strip,
   warm-up sets, the progression rule, the plate calculator, in roughly
   that order.

The watch, voice and the Swedish locale on a real device are the next
things to test the same way.
